## Project Overview

This repository contains selected work completed for the **Skill Nexis
Digital Marketing Internship**. The work covers digital-marketing
research and content/campaign activities, including competitor research,
student/community research, keyword and search-intent research, and
promotional content.

The repository contains the student's submitted/working materials. It
does not claim to include separate work completed by other members of
the partnership team.

## Work Included

### Week 1 --- Digital Marketing Research

The Week 1 work covers:

-   **Competitor Research** --- research on relevant competitors such as
    Internshala, Unstop, Great Learning, Simplilearn, and Coursera,
    including their offerings, target audiences, strengths, gaps, and
    learnings for Skill Nexis.
-   **Student Community Research** --- identification of student,
    developer, and technology communities relevant for awareness,
    outreach, partnerships, workshops, and career opportunities.
-   **Keyword Research** --- keywords organised by search intent, target
    audience, and possible Skill Nexis content ideas. The research
    materials use Google autocomplete/Google Trends as validation
    sources and avoid unsupported search-volume claims.
-   **Marketing/Content Ideas** --- student-focused content ideas such
    as internship guidance, resume preparation, interview preparation,
    skill roadmaps, and project-related content.

### Week 2 --- Content / Promotional Campaign

The Week 2 material contains promotional creative work focused on
communicating the value of Skill Nexis internships through practical
learning, project experience, industry-relevant skills, and
career-focused development.

One documented creative theme is:

> **TURN YOUR SKILLS INTO REAL EXPERIENCE**

The work targets college students, freshers, and learners looking for
practical experience and uses a clear student-focused call-to-action.
